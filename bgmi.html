Tournament Rules

Team Formation

Teams must consist of 4 players with 1 substitute allowed. All team members must be registered before the tournament starts.

Match Duration

Each match lasts approximately 30-35 minutes. Teams must be ready 15 minutes before match time.

Scoring System

Points awarded for placement and eliminations. Placement points: 1st=10pts, 2nd=6pts, 3rd=5pts, etc.

Fair Play

Cheating, hacking, or unsportsmanlike conduct is strictly prohibited and will result in immediate disqualification.
